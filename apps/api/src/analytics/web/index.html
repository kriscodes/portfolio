How these estimates work

Active time. The collector checks visibility and recent pointer, key, or scroll activity. Hidden and idle time is excluded; browser suspension and missed final requests can undercount. Cumulative snapshots avoid adding retries twice.

Visits & privacy. Random identifiers rotate daily. Visits expire after 30 minutes of inactivity and at the day boundary. Shared identifiers stay within this domain; returning people are not measured.

Coverage. Historical totals remain available below. New timing, scroll, path, and cross-site metrics begin with the updated collector. Missing data is never reconstructed from old totals.

Delivery. Blockers, privacy settings, multiple tabs, clock differences, and delayed events affect estimates. Events can retry for 24 hours. Reports may change as they arrive.

Choose an approved campaign label. Portfolio links stay short; other campaigns include tracking tags. Older custom links still attribute their source, but unrecognized campaign labels are no longer collected.

How these links work

The short portfolio links redirect to your website with a platform tag. Paste the whole link into your profile, bio, post, or message. Custom campaign links include their tags directly. This is especially useful for WhatsApp and other apps that may not send referral information.

If someone forwards a tagged link elsewhere, it keeps the original platform label. The label records the link’s source; it doesn’t reveal a visitor’s identity or prove which app they used.

A useful picture, with less personal data.

The dashboard shows aggregate traffic and actions. It doesn’t show visitor names, email addresses, exact locations, or session recordings. Page routes and campaign labels use approved catalogs. No query strings, fragments, tool inputs, prompts, names, emails, or precise locations are collected. Events are retained for 180 days; hosting backups have separate retention. Shared random identifiers expire daily, and visits expire after 30 minutes of inactivity. There is no returning-person metric.

The recent visitor count means visitors with recorded activity in the last five minutes. A LinkedIn click is an outbound click, not confirmation that someone contacted you.
