# Second-pass UX review

Reviewed and implemented 2026-09-13. Direction preserved: **Good systems. Wild places.**

## What was missing

The first design established taste, coherence, and a clear personality. Its deeper engineering explanation lived inside the Care IQ case study, while the homepage interactions were familiar. A reader who had not opened the report could miss the implementation skill. The page also needed a more explicit experience signal and a tighter route from introduction to evidence.

## The changes

The hero now says **10+ years in software. Still curious.** This uses Kristian’s description of his experience. The primary button moves directly to selected work on the same page. A quieter secondary link offers the Wind Study immediately to visitors who want to explore.

The reading order is **introduction → selected work → field notes → personal perspective and Wind Study → contact**. Grouping the work and thinking builds professional credibility before the longer personal interlude. The contact panel remains the unmistakable ending.

A sticky chapter index gives visitors their position and direct routes between Work, Notes, and Outside. All three fit on a narrow phone screen. Desktop includes a contact shortcut. Native anchors preserve familiar navigation and move focus to the selected section.

The Care IQ feature now has explicit destinations: the title and field-report link open the full case study; the architecture diagram opens its request walkthrough. The entire surrounding card no longer pretends to be one undifferentiated button.

## Why the Wind Study belongs

The public Instagram grid shows sailing, wooded terrain, and candid exploration. A small flow instrument connects the sailing interest to visible frontend craft. It uses the existing paper, ink, orange, fine rules, and editorial typography, so it feels part of the field guide.

Visitors can move an eddy, change the bearing, adjust the strength, and watch hundreds of paths respond. The compass moves with the controls. A short optional explanation makes the engineering legible without interrupting the personal tone. It is an expressive interaction, not a weather product.

The interaction also has practical polish: native inputs, keyboard movement, pause, reset, a still mode for reduced motion, bounded rendering work, and suspension when out of view. These choices demonstrate care as well as visual technique.

## What still supplies seniority

An interactive component demonstrates frontend execution; a leadership portfolio also needs specific evidence of ownership and judgment. The next editorial addition should be a compact record of one consequential system, Kristian’s personal scope, the people involved, a difficult tradeoff, and a verified result. Do not infer team size, commercial impact, titles, or dates from years of experience.

Original photography and one strong leadership example are the highest-value additions. Preserve the current restraint as that evidence arrives.
